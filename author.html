Behind the book

About the author.

Allison Cohen

Allison Cohen is a private academic coach based in Manhattan, where she lives with her husband and two Bengal cats, Denner and Mochi. Before moving to New York City, she taught seventh-grade math in New Jersey for five years, after teaching martial arts for many years.

Allison has worked privately with hundreds of students and prepares children for school entrance exams, including the ISEE, SSAT, SHSAT, TACHS, and HSPT. She also guides families through school selection, student and parent statements, and interview preparation. She provides academic support and enrichment across subjects for elementary and middle school students, specializing in math and writing. At the high school level, she works with student on writing, editing, organization, and study skills.

Allison holds an undergraduate degree from NYU and a master’s degree from Monmouth University.
